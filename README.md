Professional Business Support Chatbot

Overview

Professional Business Support Chatbot is a Python-based customer support and business assistance platform designed to simulate real-world customer service operations. The project focuses on conversation management, customer record handling, business knowledge management, escalation tracking, analytics, and persistent data storage.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate practical software engineering skills, including Python development, database design, application architecture, data management, and user-focused business solutions.
